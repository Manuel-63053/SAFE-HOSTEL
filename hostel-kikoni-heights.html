About this hostel

Kikoni Heights is a four-storey hostel with a walled compound, a manned gate and CCTV on every floor. Rooms have a reading desk, wardrobe and a window that opens. Water is stored in two tanks so supply continues during cuts, and solar backup keeps lights and sockets on at night.
