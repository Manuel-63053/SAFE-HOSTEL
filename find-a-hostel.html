How to use the search

  1. Set your budget

    Prices are rent per semester in Uganda shillings. Choose the most you can afford.

  2. Choose distance and gender

    Pick how far you are happy to walk to lectures, and whether you want a female, male or mixed hostel.

  3. Pick a room type

    Single, double, triple or self-contained. Shared rooms are cheaper; self-contained rooms have a private bathroom.

  4. Open a hostel and book a viewing

    Compare photos, facilities, the map and reviews. Then visit before paying.
