5.1 · How we verify

Behind the badge

The verified badge means a real person from our team has checked the hostel's ownership, safety and rooms.

See the rules →

Our verification process

It takes up to ten working days from registration to a live listing.

Verified

  1. Owner registers

    The owner or manager submits hostel details, photos and proof of ownership or management.

  2. Document check

    We confirm the documents are genuine and the names match, and call the owner.

  3. Site visit

    Two team members visit unannounced within an agreed week, and check the fence, gate, guard, water, power, fire safety and rooms.

  4. Decision

    If every rule is met, the hostel goes live with the badge. If not, we explain what must be fixed.

  5. Every semester

    We re-check prices and vacancies, and visit again if there are complaints.

  6. Reports

    If students report a scam or unsafe conditions, we suspend the listing while we investigate.

What the badge means

We met the owner or manager, saw their documents, and the hostel met our safety rules on the day we visited.

What it doesn't mean

It is not a guarantee. Always view the room and follow the checklist before paying.

Never paid to us

Owners never pay for the badge, so it can't be bought.
