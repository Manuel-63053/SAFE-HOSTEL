Questions, answered

The ten questions students and owners ask us most.

Is SafeHostel free for students?

Yes. Searching, booking viewings and reporting scams is completely free. We never ask students for money.

What does the verified badge mean?

Our team has met the owner or manager, checked their documents and visited the hostel to check security, water, power, fire safety and the rooms. Read how we verify.

Do I pay SafeHostel to book a viewing?

No. Booking a viewing is free. Pay rent only to the hostel's official account, and only after you have viewed the room.

Someone says they are a SafeHostel agent. Are they?

SafeHostel does not use agents and will never ask you for money. Report them on our report a scam page.

How are prices shown?

All prices are rent per semester in Uganda shillings, as confirmed with the hostel during verification. Always confirm the price at your viewing.

Can my parents use the site to choose for me?

Yes. Parents and guardians can browse hostels, read reviews and request a viewing on your behalf.

How do I list my hostel?

Fill in the registration form with your details, photos and proof of ownership or management. Listing is free.

How long does verification take?

Usually up to ten working days, depending on when we can agree a site visit.

Are the reviews real?

Reviews come from students who say they live or have lived in the hostel. We check each one before publishing and remove fake or abusive reviews.

I paid and the room doesn't exist. What now?

Report it to the police immediately (999 / 112), keep all messages and receipts, and tell us so we can warn others.
