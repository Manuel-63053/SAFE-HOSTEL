3.2 · Verification rules

What we check

A hostel only gets the verified badge if it meets every rule below. We re-check each listing every semester.

Register →
1

Ownership

We see a land title, lease or a signed management letter, and the national ID of the person who registered. The name must match.

2

Secure fence and gate

The compound is fully fenced or walled, with one or more lockable gates.

3

Security guard

A guard is on duty at the gate at night (24 hours preferred) and visitors are recorded.

4

Water and power

Running water or a stored supply, and a working electricity connection. We note any backup power.

5

Fire safety

At least one working fire extinguisher per floor, and corridors and exits kept clear.

6

Rooms match the photos

Photos are recent and show the actual rooms on offer. Prices match what students will be charged.

7

Official payment account

Rent is paid into an account in the hostel's or owner's name, and receipts are given for every payment.

8

Respect for tenants

Rules are written down and shared, and there is no demand for more than one semester in advance.

Documents to have ready

Upload clear photos or PDFs in the registration form. Only our verification team can see them.
