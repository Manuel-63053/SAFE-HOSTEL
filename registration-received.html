Thank you. Your hostel is now waiting for verification. It will not appear in search results until our team has checked it.

What happens next

  1. Document check (2–3 days)

    We check your ID and proof of ownership or management. We may call to ask for more information.

  2. We agree a visit

    We call you on the number you gave to agree a date for the site visit.

  3. Site visit

    Two members of our team visit to check the rooms, safety and facilities against our verification rules.

  4. Your listing goes live

    Once approved, your hostel appears with the verified badge and you start receiving viewing requests.
