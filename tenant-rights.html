4.2 · Your rights

Your rights as a tenant

Students renting hostel rooms are tenants. Here are the basics, in plain language.

₪

A receipt for every payment

You have the right to a receipt for rent and any deposit you pay. Keep every receipt until you move out.

✎

A clear agreement

Ask for the rent, what it covers, the deposit and the rules in writing before you pay.

⌂

A safe, usable room

The landlord should keep the room and building in a safe condition and carry out repairs that are their responsibility.

⚿

Privacy

The landlord or manager should give notice before entering your room, except in an emergency.

↗

Notice before changes

Rent increases and eviction must follow proper notice. You cannot simply be locked out or have your things removed.

↺

Your deposit back

A deposit should be returned when you leave, minus only the cost of damage you caused beyond normal wear.

Where this comes from

In Uganda, renting is governed by the Landlord and Tenant Act, 2022. Among other things, it limits how much rent a landlord can demand in advance, requires rent to be charged in Uganda shillings, and sets out how notice and evictions must be handled.

This page is a simple summary to help students, not legal advice. If you have a serious dispute, contact your university's dean of students office or a legal aid clinic.
