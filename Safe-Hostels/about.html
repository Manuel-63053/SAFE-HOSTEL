5.0 · About Us

Why we exist

Every intake, thousands of first-years have days to find a room. Too many pay strangers for rooms that don't exist, or move into hostels nobody has checked.

Our mission

One trusted place for safe student housing.

SafeHostel brings verified hostel information into one place, so that students and parents don't have to depend on posters, strangers or "agents".

Police report that scams targeting new students are a weekly occurrence, and investigations have found hostels operating without guards, fences or known owners. Universities only supervise the hostels officially affiliated with them, so most students are on their own.

We visit and check every hostel before it is listed, publish honest reviews from residents, and give students a simple way to report scams and unsafe buildings.
