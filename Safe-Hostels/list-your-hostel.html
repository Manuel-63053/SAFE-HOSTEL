From form to verified in four steps

Most hostels are verified within ten working days of registering.

  1. Register online

    Fill in your details and upload photos and proof of ownership or management.

  2. Document check

  3. Site visit

    Two members of our team visit to check safety, rooms and facilities.

    Your hostel appears in search results with the verified badge.
