Viewing requested

We have passed your request to the hostel's verified manager. Keep your phone close: they will call you to confirm the date and time.

What happens next

  1. Wait for the call

    The verified manager will call within one working day to confirm. The number they call from is shown on the hostel's page.

  2. Visit the hostel

    Go at the agreed time, preferably with a friend or parent. Check the room, water, power, locks and the gate guard.

  3. Do not pay before viewing

    Never send money before you have seen the room and met the manager. Pay only into the hostel's official account and ask for a receipt.

  4. Something feels wrong?

    If anyone asks you for money first, stop and report it to us straight away.
