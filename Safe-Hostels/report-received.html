Report received

Thank you for speaking up. Your report helps protect the next student.

What happens next

  1. If you lost money, go to the police

    Report it at the nearest police station or call 999 / 112. Take your receipts, messages and the numbers used.

  2. We review it within 48 hours

    If a listed hostel is involved, we suspend its listing while we investigate.

  3. We warn other students

    We share warnings (without your name) with student leaders and welfare offices.
