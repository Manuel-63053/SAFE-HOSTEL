Privacy

Privacy notice

How SafeHostel collects, uses and protects your personal information.

What we collect

We only collect what you give us in our forms: your name and phone number when you book a viewing; your name and review when you write one; your details, hostel information, photos and documents when you register a hostel; and whatever you choose to include in a message or scam report.

How we use it

  • Viewing requests are shared with the verified manager of that hostel so they can contact you.
  • Owner documents are seen only by our verification team and are never published.
  • Scam reports are used to investigate and warn other students. We never publish your name.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email hello@safehostel.ug.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics.
