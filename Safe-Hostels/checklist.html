4.1 · Checklist

Before you pay

Go through every step. If any of them fails, do not pay; walk away and look at another hostel.

The 8-step checklist

Screenshot this page or print it and take it with you to viewings.

Find verified hostels →

  1. View the room in person

    Never pay for a room you have only seen in photos or a WhatsApp video. Visit it, preferably with a friend or parent.

  2. Meet the real owner or manager

    Ask for their name and national ID, and check it matches the verified listing on SafeHostel.

  3. Check the security

    Is there a full fence, a lockable gate and a guard at night? Do the room door and windows lock?

  4. Test water and power

    Open a tap, switch on a light and ask what happens during load shedding or water cuts.

  5. Look for fire safety

    There should be a working fire extinguisher on each floor and a clear way out.

  6. Read the rules and agreement

    Ask for the hostel rules and a written tenancy agreement before you pay. Read it all.

  7. Pay into the official account

    Pay into a bank or mobile-money account in the hostel's or owner's name, never a 'friend's' number.

  8. Get a receipt every time

    Ask for a written or printed receipt showing the amount, date, room and who received the money.

Warning signs

How to spot a fake agent

Conmen often befriend new students in their first week. If you see any of these signs, stop and check.

Report a scam →

  • They approach you firstSomeone at the gate, in a taxi park or on social media 'knows a cheap hostel'.
  • They want money nowA 'booking fee' or 'deposit' before you have seen the room, to 'secure' it.
  • The price is too goodSelf-contained rooms near campus for far below everyone else's price.
  • They avoid the ownerYou cannot meet the manager, or the 'owner' is always travelling.
  • Pay to a personal numberThey ask you to pay to a personal mobile-money number, not the hostel's account.
  • No receiptThey promise to 'bring the receipt later', or refuse to write one.
