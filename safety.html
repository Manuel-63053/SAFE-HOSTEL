Everyday safety

⌂

Know your exits

On your first night, find the fire exits and the extinguisher on your floor.

⚿

Lock up

Lock your room even for short trips, and keep valuables in a lockable box.

☾

Walk in groups

After dark, walk with friends on lit roads and avoid shortcuts.

☏

Share your address

Send your hostel name, room and manager's number to a parent or guardian.
